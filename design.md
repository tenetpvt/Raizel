# Raizel Design System: Lucent Arcana

## Brand & Style Philosophy
Raizel translates personal progression and real-world mastery into an elevated, technical RPG experience. It rejects high-saturation fantasy tropes, pixel-art clichés, and noisy gamification textures in favor of restrained obsidian minimalism, optical glass treatments, and razor-sharp data density.

The aesthetic fuses the premium restraint of precision hardware presentation with the surgical utility of high-end developer interfaces. The interface acts as a silent mirror of personal momentum: default states remain deep, cold, and quiet, while achievements, kinetic streaks, and stat progressions radiate subtle, crystalline photonic energy (**"earned luminescence"**).

## Color Palette

### Base Surfaces
- **Canvas / Background**: `#0A0A0F` (unyielding deep obsidian)
- **Base Surfaces**: `#141420`
- **Elevated Surfaces**: `#1C1C2E` (cards, containers)
- **Higher Elevation / Modals**: `#202030`
- **Outlines / Hairline Dividers**: `#2A2D38` / `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`

### Typography
- **Primary Text**: `#F5F5F7` (high-clarity white with faint cool undertone)
- **Secondary / Meta Text**: `#9B9BA8` (neutral mid-tone for telemetry & labels)
- **Tertiary / Inactive**: `#5E5E6D`

### RPG Attributes & Earned Luminescence
- **Intellect (Primary Kinetic Engine)**: `#4DD8FF` (Cyan) — Deep work sprints, learning, state metrics
- **Discipline (Secondary Aura)**: `#9D7BFF` (Violet) — Unbroken morning routines, fasting, mastery
- **Strength (Kinetic Force)**: `#FF6B4A` (Ember Orange) — Resistance training, compound loading, high heat
- **Vitality (Constitution & Vitality)**: `#34D399` (Emerald) — Zone 2 endurance, recovery, sleep
- **Stardust (Prestige & Economy)**: `#F2B84B` (Stardust Amber) — Currency, legendary unlocks, milestone achievements

---

## Typography Hierarchy
- **Display & Section Titles**: `Syne` / `Geist` bold tracking-tight
- **Body & High-Density Telemetry**: `Geist` / `Inter`
- **Micro-labels & Instrumentation**: `Geist Mono` uppercase, tracking `0.08em`, tabular numerals (`tnum`)

---

## Motion & Interaction Principles
- **Light as Feedback**: Stars and lines illuminate upon task completion rather than being decorative clutter.
- **Lenis Smooth Scrolling**: Weighted, fluid scrolling experience.
- **Micro-Springs**: Tactile, dampened springs on quest completions and button presses.
- **Accessibility**: Strict respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
